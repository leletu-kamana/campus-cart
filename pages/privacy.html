Privacy Policy

This policy explains what personal information Campus-Cart handles, why we handle it, who sees it and how you can control it. It is written to follow South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA).

Last updated: 12 October 2026 • Version 1.0

Demonstration notice. Campus-Cart is a fictional organisation built as a student website project at The IIE Rosebank International. No real goods are sold, no payments are taken and no personal information is kept on a server. This page explains how the website works today and how personal information and orders would be handled if Campus-Cart begins trading.

1. Who we are

Campus-Cart (“we”, “us”) is a South African student essentials marketplace. For the purposes of POPIA, Campus-Cart is the responsible party for personal information collected through this website.

  • Head office: 128 Lynnwood Road, Hatfield, Pretoria, 0083.
  • Email: hello@campus-cart.co.za.
  • Phone and WhatsApp: 012 345 6789 (Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 17:00 SAST).
  • Information Officer: the Campus-Cart owner. Send privacy requests to the email address above with the subject “Privacy request”.

2. What personal information we handle

  • Enquiry and contact forms. Your name, email address, the product or topic, quantity, delivery or collection choice, province, institution, an optional order reference, an optional phone or WhatsApp number and your message. These forms open your own email app (a mailto: link), so the details travel from your email account to ours. This website does not receive or store them on a server.
  • Order summary. Items you add are kept in your browser's session storage so the Account page can show them. They stay on your device and are removed when you close the tab. We only receive them if you choose to send the summary to us by email or WhatsApp.
  • Emails and WhatsApp messages. Whatever you send us, such as your name, contact details, delivery address or collection point and proof of payment.
  • Technical information. When a page loads, our host (GitHub Pages) and any embedded service you allow can see your IP address, browser type and the pages you request.
  • Saved choices. Your cookie choices and your best score in the 404 game are saved on your own device only.

We do not ask for identity numbers, card details, health information or other special personal information. Please do not send them to us.

3. Why we use it and our lawful basis

  • To reply to enquiries and support requests (your request, and our legitimate interest in answering it).
  • To prepare, confirm, deliver or collect orders and to arrange refunds (to perform our agreement with you).
  • To keep records we are by law to keep, such as financial and tax records (legal obligation).
  • To keep the website secure and working (legitimate interest).
  • To load embedded maps, only after you allow them (your consent).

We only use personal information for the purpose it was given for, and we do not use it for unrelated purposes without asking you first.

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell personal information. We share it only where needed to run the service:

  • Couriers and collection partners receive the name, phone number and address needed to deliver your order.
  • Email and messaging services (your email provider and WhatsApp) carry the messages you send us.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website and keeps ordinary server logs.
  • Google (embedded map) and the OpenStreetMap Foundation (map tiles) receive technical information, but only after you allow maps.
  • LinkedIn serves the profile photo on the About page, so it can see your IP address when that page loads.
  • Our bank and your bank handle EFT payments and refunds.
  • The authorities, if the law requires us to disclose information.

5. Transfers outside South Africa

Some of the services above, such as GitHub, Google and WhatsApp, process information outside South Africa. We only use providers that are bound by law, a binding agreement or their own rules to protect personal information to a standard comparable to POPIA, or we ask for your consent first.

6. How long we keep it

  • Enquiries and support emails: for as long as needed to deal with your request, and normally no longer than 12 months afterwards.
  • Order, payment and refund records: normally five years, because tax and consumer law require records to be kept.
  • Browser storage on your device: until you clear it, as explained in the Cookie Policy.

When information is no longer needed we delete it or make it anonymous.

7. How we protect it

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information, including secure (HTTPS) connections, limiting who can see orders and enquiries, and keeping payment details out of the website entirely. If we ever have reason to believe that personal information has been accessed without authority, we will tell the Information Regulator and the people affected as POPIA requires.

8. Your rights

Under POPIA you may:

  • ask whether we hold personal information about you and ask for a copy;
  • ask us to correct or delete personal information that is wrong, out of date, incomplete or no longer needed;
  • object to us using your personal information, and withdraw consent you gave us earlier;
  • complain to the Information Regulator (see section 13).

To use any of these rights, email hello@campus-cart.co.za. We may ask you to prove who you are so that nobody else can obtain your information, and we aim to respond within 30 days.

9. Children

This website is meant for students and their parents or guardians. If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian to place orders and send enquiries for you. We do not knowingly collect personal information about children without the consent of a parent or guardian.

10. Marketing messages

We do not send unsolicited marketing. We will only send you promotional messages if you have clearly agreed to receive them, and every message will explain how to opt out.

11. Cookies and similar technologies

The website uses only the browser storage it needs to work, and it loads embedded maps only after you allow them. See the Cookie Policy for the full list, and use the Cookie settings link in the footer to change your choices at any time.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the website or the law changes. The date at the top shows when it was last updated. If a change is important, we will say so on the website.

13. Complaints

If you think we have not handled your personal information properly, please contact us first so we can try to put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Regulator:

  • The Information Regulator (South Africa), JD House, 27 Stiemens Street, Braamfontein, Johannesburg, 2001.
  • Website: inforegulator.org.za.
  • POPIA complaints: POPIAComplaints@inforegulator.org.za.
  • General enquiries: Enquiries@inforegulator.org.za.

Contact details can change, so please check the Regulator's website for the latest.
