Know exactly what you’re getting

The same way SoleStyle gives shoe shoppers fit confidence, Campus-Cart gives parents and guardians item-level confidence before registration week.

  • Fixed contents. Every bundle lists each item and its total price before you order.

  • Reviewed listings. Second-hand items are manually checked by our team before they go live.

  • Safe collection. Residence desks, campus points or secure lockers — never a random street corner.

  • EFT only. No card details are ever handled on this site.

Campus-Cart began when a group of students resold their res kit instead of throwing it away. Today we serve students aged 19–30 and the parents and guardians who fund them, across major South African campuses.
