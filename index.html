About us

Started in a res corridor in 2021

Campus-Cart began when a group of students resold their res kit instead of throwing it away. Today we serve students aged 19–30 and the parents and guardians who fund them, across major South African campuses.

Read our story

Who we serve

Primary: tertiary students aged 19–30 living in residence, digs or at home.

Secondary: parents and guardians budgeting for registration season.
