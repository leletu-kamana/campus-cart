Returns, Refunds and Delivery

This page explains how your order reaches you and what you can do if you change your mind or something is wrong. It forms part of our Terms and Conditions.

Last updated: 12 October 2026 • Version 1.0

Demonstration notice. Campus-Cart is a fictional organisation built as a student website project at The IIE Rosebank International. No real goods are sold, no payments are taken and no personal information is kept on a server. This page explains how the website works today and how personal information and orders would be handled if Campus-Cart begins trading.

1. Delivery and collection

  • You can choose delivery to an address or collection from a Campus-Cart collection point. The collection points are listed on the contact page.
  • We confirm the delivery fee, if there is one, and the expected delivery or collection date when we confirm your order.
  • Delivery times are estimates. Delays caused by couriers, weather or power cuts are not always in our control, but we will keep you informed.
  • Please check your items when you receive them and tell us within 48 hours if anything is missing or damaged, so we can sort it out quickly.

2. Changing your mind

If you buy from us online, the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act gives you the right to cancel an order without giving a reason within seven days after the goods are delivered to you. To use this right:

  • email orders@campus-cart.co.za within the seven days with your order reference;
  • send the item back unused, in the condition you received it and in its original packaging where possible;
  • we will refund what you paid, less the direct cost of returning the goods unless the law says otherwise, within 30 days of your cancellation.

Some goods cannot be returned for this reason, such as items made to your order and, for hygiene reasons, bedding or personal items once they have been opened or used, unless they are faulty.

3. Faulty or wrongly described goods

Under the Consumer Protection Act you have the right to goods that work as they should and are as described. If an item is faulty, unsafe or not as described, tell us within six months of delivery. You may choose to have it repaired, replaced or refunded, and we will cover the cost of returning it. This also applies to second-hand goods, which we describe as honestly as we can.

4. How to return an item

  • Email orders@campus-cart.co.za or message us on WhatsApp on 012 345 6789 with your order reference and a short description, and a photo if the item is faulty.
  • We will tell you how to return it, by courier or by handing it in at a collection point.
  • Keep your proof of postage or hand-in until you have your refund.

5. Refunds

  • Refunds are paid by EFT into the account you paid from, or another account you ask us to use.
  • We aim to refund within seven working days of receiving and checking the returned item, and in any case within 30 days.
  • If an item is out of stock after you have paid, we will refund you in full or offer a replacement you accept.

6. If you sold an item through us

If an item you sold is returned because it was not as you described, we may reverse the sale and ask you to collect the item. We will always explain the reason first.

7. Contact us

Email orders@campus-cart.co.za, message us on WhatsApp on 012 345 6789, or use the contact page. We are available Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 17:00 SAST.

These rules sit alongside your legal rights and do not take any of them away. For more information about consumer rights, you can visit the National Consumer Commission at thencc.gov.za.
