## 3. What we offer

Campus-Cart is a marketplace for new and second-hand student essentials, such as bedding, appliances, stationery, furniture, textbooks and starter bundles. The website lets you browse products, build an order summary and send it to us. You do not need an account to use the website.

## 4. Orders and how a contract is formed

- Your order summary is a request, not a purchase. A binding agreement exists only when we confirm your order to you in writing, by email or WhatsApp.
- All items are subject to availability. If an item is no longer available we will tell you and offer a replacement or a full refund of anything you have paid for it.
- We may decline or cancel an order if there is a pricing error, if we cannot deliver to your area or if we have reason to believe the order is fraudulent. If you have already paid, we will refund you in full.
- Please check your details carefully. If something is wrong, tell us as soon as possible so we can fix it.

## 5. Prices and payment

- Prices are shown in South African rand (ZAR). Where Campus-Cart is registered for VAT, prices include VAT.
- Delivery fees, if any, are shown or confirmed before you pay.
- Payment is by electronic funds transfer (EFT). Use your order reference as the payment reference and email your proof of payment to [orders@campus-cart.co.za](mailto:orders@campus-cart.co.za). Your order is released once the payment has cleared.
- We never ask for card numbers, PINs or passwords. Please do not send them.
