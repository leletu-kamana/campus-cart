Cookie Policy

This policy explains what is stored on your device when you use Campus-Cart, which outside services can see your visit, and how to control them.

Last updated: 12 October 2026 • Version 1.0

Demonstration notice. Campus-Cart is a fictional organisation built as a student website project at The IIE Rosebank International. No real goods are sold, no payments are taken and no personal information is kept on a server. This page explains how the website works today and how personal information and orders would be handled if Campus-Cart begins trading.

  2. 2 Storage used by Campus-Cart

1. What this policy covers

Cookies are small files that a website or an embedded service stores on your device. Browsers also offer similar tools called local storage and session storage. This policy covers all of them. The Campus-Cart website does not set any cookies of its own. It uses browser storage, listed below, and a few outside services that may set cookies.

We do not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels or website analytics.

2. Storage used by Campus-Cart

Browser storage set by the Campus-Cart website
Name Where it is kept What it does How long it lasts Type
cc_order Session storage Keeps the items in your order summary while you browse. Until you close the tab. Essential
cc_consent Local storage Remembers your cookie choices and the date you made them. Up to 12 months, or until you clear your browser data. Essential
cc_game_best Local storage Remembers your best score in the 404 game. It is only saved if you play. Until you clear your browser data. Functional

Essential storage is needed for the website to work, so it does not need your consent. We ask for your choice before using anything else.

3. Outside services

Third-party services that may see your visit
Service Where it appears What it does What it can see When it loads
Google Maps Shop page, collection map Shows an embedded map. Your IP address and browser details. It may set cookies. Only after you allow maps.
OpenStreetMap Contact page, interactive map Supplies the map tiles. Your IP address and browser details. Only after you allow maps.
LinkedIn About page, profile photo Serves the photo. Your IP address and browser details. When the About page loads.
GitHub Pages Every page Hosts the website. Your IP address and browser details in its server logs. On every visit.
WhatsApp Send via WhatsApp links Opens a chat with us. Whatever you send, if you choose to. Only when you click the link.

These services have their own privacy and cookie policies, which we do not control.

4. Your choices

  • When you first visit, a banner lets you accept all, choose essential only, or pick settings.

  • You can change your mind at any time with the Cookie settings button in the footer of every page. Switching maps off takes full effect when you reload the page.

  • Your browser can also block or delete cookies and site data. If you block storage, the order summary may not stay saved between pages.

  • We ask for your choices again after 12 months.

5. More information

See our Privacy Policy to find out how we handle personal information, or email hello@campus-cart.co.za if you have a question.

We may update this policy when the website changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.
