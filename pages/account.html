Student path

If you are the student

  1. 1

    Browse Shop New for basics you need immediately.

  2. 2

    Check the Second-Hand Marketplace before buying new — desks, fridges and textbooks are often half price.

  3. 3

    Use Deals filters to sort by price.

  4. 4

    Add items to your Order Summary below and pick a collection point.

  5. 5

    Hand off by WhatsApp or email; we confirm availability and payment plan.

  6. 6

    Selling at year end? See Sell With Us.

Good to know

Collection is free at listed residence, campus and locker points. Bring your student card for handover.

Payment plans are arranged by email — nothing is charged on this website.

Parent / Guardian path

If you are the parent or guardian

  1. 1

    Print the First Year Checklist and tick off what you already own.

  2. 2

    Compare the Basic, Standard and Premium bundles — contents and prices are fixed.

  3. 3

    Send a pre-purchase enquiry to confirm availability and a payment plan.

  4. 4

    Add the bundle to the Order Summary below and choose collection or delivery.

  5. 5

    Pay by EFT using our static banking details, or arrange over WhatsApp.

Budget guidance

Most first-year families spend between R1 500 and R4 500 on res essentials. Choosing second-hand for furniture and textbooks typically saves 30–50%.
