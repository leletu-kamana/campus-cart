Coverage

Every province. Public and private campuses.

Campus-Cart serves all nine provinces: 26 public universities, 50 public TVET colleges and the major private institutions and colleges. Teacher education is included through the education faculties of the public universities and private teacher institutes such as Embury (STADIO).

Hub addresses, times and delivery estimates are illustrative because Campus-Cart is a fictional business. Private institutions listed here are the major providers. If yours is missing, choose "My institution is not listed" on the enquiry form and we deliver there too. Check any private institution's registration on the DHET register before you enrol.
