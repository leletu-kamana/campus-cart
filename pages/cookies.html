Cookie Policy

This policy explains what is stored on your device when you use Campus-Cart, which outside services can see your visit, and how to control them.

Last updated: 12 October 2026 · Version 1.0

Demonstration notice. Campus-Cart is a fictional organisation built as a student website project at The IIE Rosebank International. No real goods are sold, no payments are taken and no personal information is kept on a server. This page explains how the website works today and how personal information and orders would be handled if Campus-Cart begins trading.

  2. Storage used by Campus-Cart

1. What this policy covers

Cookies are small files that a website or an embedded service stores on your device. Browsers also offer similar tools called local storage and session storage. This policy covers all of them. The Campus-Cart website does not set any cookies of its own. It uses browser storage, listed below, and a few outside services that may set cookies.

We do not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels or website analytics.

4. Your choices

  • When you first visit, a banner lets you accept all, choose essential only, or pick settings.
  • You can change your mind at any time with the Cookie settings button in the footer of every page. Switching maps off takes full effect when you reload the page.
  • Your browser can also block or delete cookies and site data. If you block storage, the order summary may not stay saved between pages.
  • We ask for your choices again after 12 months.
