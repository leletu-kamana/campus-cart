About us

Built by students, for students and their families

Campus-Cart is a South African student essentials marketplace that pairs affordable new stock with a manually reviewed second-hand range.

Our history

In 2021 a handful of final-year students in Pretoria noticed the same thing every November: perfectly good kettles, fridges, desks and textbooks were being dumped in res corridors, while first-years arriving in January were paying full retail for the same items.

What began as a WhatsApp group with a spreadsheet grew into an organised handover service across three campuses. By 2023 we had added new stock so that families could buy a complete bundle in one go, and by 2025 we formalised collection points at residences, campus desks and secure lockers.

Today Campus-Cart operates as a small, student-run team with reviewers who physically check every second-hand listing before it goes live.

Our mission

To make the cost of starting tertiary education fairer, by giving students and their families access to affordable, quality-checked essentials and a safe way to resell what they outgrow.

Our vision

A South Africa where no student delays or drops out of study because they cannot afford a bed, a desk or a prescribed textbook — and where student kit circulates instead of ending up in a skip.

Audience

Who we serve

Primary audience

Tertiary students, ages 19–30

Undergraduates, postgraduates and TVET students living in residence, private digs or commuting from home. They are price-sensitive, mobile-first, and often buying for the first time without family nearby.

  • Need affordable basics fast, usually in registration week
  • Want to resell items at the end of the year
  • Prefer collection on or near campus
Student path
Secondary audience

Parents and guardians

Adults funding a student's first year who need clear, fixed pricing, a checklist they can print, and reassurance that a second-hand item has been checked by a person.

  • Budget in advance with fixed-price bundles
  • Larger type, plain language, printable checklist
  • Direct contact by email or WhatsApp
