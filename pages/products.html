Frequently asked questions

For students

Answers about shopping, payments, second-hand items, delivery and selling on Campus-Cart.

Can I pay in instalments?

Payment plans are arranged manually over email before collection. Typically a 50% holding amount and the balance on collection. Nothing is charged on this website.

What if a second-hand item is faulty?

Report it within 48 hours of collection and we arrange a swap or refund with the seller. Items are tested at review, so faults are rare.

How does delivery work?

Collection at any listed residence, campus or locker point is free. Courier delivery is quoted per order and confirmed before you pay.

How much do I earn when selling?

You set the asking price. Campus-Cart keeps a small handling fee that is confirmed in writing before your listing goes live.

For parents and guardians

Answers about bundle prices, paying for a student, returns, stock and seller checks.

Are bundle prices fixed?

Yes. Each bundle lists its contents and one price. Substitutions on request never increase the price without written confirmation.

Can I pay off a bundle before registration?

Yes — arrange a staged payment plan by email. We hold stock once the first payment reflects.

What is the returns policy?

Unused new items may be returned within 7 days of collection in their original packaging. Second-hand items follow the 48-hour fault window.

How do I know a seller is legitimate?

Every second-hand listing is inspected in person by our team before publication. The Verified Seller badge means a staff member handled the item.
